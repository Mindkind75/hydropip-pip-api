# HydroPip Troubleshooting

Pip should troubleshoot the HydroPip timed-feed tower system before giving generic hydroponic advice.

## Tower Is Dry

Likely causes:

- Feed duration is too short.
- Feed windows are too far apart.
- Main hose feed hole is clogged or too small.
- Small feed tube is kinked.
- Diffuser tube is blocked or misplaced.
- Pump flow is weak.
- Media has dried and is resisting even wetting.

Next checks:

1. Confirm the feed pump turns on.
2. Confirm water reaches the tower top.
3. Remove the small tube and check flow.
4. Flush the end of the main hose.
5. Check media moisture at top, middle, and lower pockets.
6. Add a short feed window only after confirming flow is even.

## Too Much Runoff

Likely causes:

- Feed duration is too long.
- Feed windows are too frequent.
- Media is already saturated.
- Diffuser sends water too directly through one path.
- Tower is not level.

Next checks:

1. Shorten feed duration before changing the build.
2. Confirm diffuser spreads flow.
3. Check if only one tower has excess runoff.
4. Compare media moisture across towers.
5. Record runoff after the next feed.

## Uneven Tower Flow

Likely causes:

- Feed hole sizes are inconsistent.
- Hose pressure drops along the line.
- Tubes are different lengths.
- Tubes or diffusers are clogged.
- End flush valve is partly open.

Next checks:

1. Flush the main hose end.
2. Inspect each feed tube.
3. Compare flow from first and last tower.
4. Keep tube lengths consistent where practical.
5. Add expansion carefully and retest after each tower.

## Plants Look Weak

Pip should ask for pictures if available, plus crop, planting date, pH, EC/TDS, feed schedule, runoff, weather, and media moisture. Pip should avoid diagnosing disease with certainty.

Common HydroPip checks:

- pH drift after nutrient mixing.
- EC/TDS too weak or too strong for crop stage.
- Feed timing not matching heat and plant size.
- Tower pockets drying between feeds.
- Roots or debris blocking flow.
- Used IBC safety or contamination concerns.

## IBC Safety Concern

Never assume a used IBC is safe. Ask what it contained before. Food-safe or known non-hazardous contents are preferred. Avoid unknown chemicals. Car wash soap totes can be a low-cost option only when prior contents are known and the tote is cleaned thoroughly.
