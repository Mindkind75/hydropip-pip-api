The system in one glance

Mix once. Feed briefly. Let each tower drain.

HydroPip uses a large mixed reservoir and scheduled feed windows. It does not route tower runoff back into the IBC.

1IBCStores mixed water and nutrients.
2Mixing pumpCirculates before readings and feeding.
3Feed pumpRuns during scheduled windows.
4Main hosePasses every tower and ends in a flush valve.
5Tower linesDeliver water at the top of each stack.
6Light runoffDrains away without returning.

There is no universal timer setting. Start with short feeds, observe one representative tower, and let Pip help tune duration using crop, weather, media moisture, and runoff.

Quick Start

Six stages from open ground to growing food.

Use this page for orientation. Track My Build holds quantities, shopping links, and checkmarks. Pip handles the question in front of you.

1

Choose and clear the site

Confirm sun, access, drainage, IBC placement, and spacing. Before driving a support, call 811 or request utility marking online.

2

Set the supports

Use one 8-10 foot, 1/2-inch galvanized steel pipe per tower. Keep roughly 5 feet above grade and anchor deeper where wind requires it.

3

Fill and stack

Fill the four-pocket sections with reusable 50/50 perlite and vermiculite, set the cinder-block base, then stack ten sections over each support.

4

Plumb and test

Secure both IBC pumps, run the main hose through the removable top guides, add one small rigid feed line per tower, flush, and confirm delivery.

5

Mix and plant

Fill the IBC, circulate nutrients before testing, transplant into moist media, and record the crop, date, pH, and EC or TDS.

6

Feed, watch, tune

Begin with short daytime feeds. Check delivery, media moisture, plant response, and runoff before changing duration or frequency.

Care rhythm

Watch the system, not the clock.

This is a starting rhythm, not a rigid prescription. Weather, crop size, source water, and reservoir level change what the system needs.

At a glance
  • Plants look upright and evenly watered.
  • Every tower receives water.
  • No leaks, dry pockets, or heavy runoff.
Regular checks
  • Review pH and EC/TDS trends.
  • Check media before increasing feed.
  • Inspect leaves, roots, pumps, and timer.
Reservoir care
  • Keep the IBC shaded and covered.
  • Circulate before taking readings.
  • Refill based on use, not a fixed date.
Crop work
  • Thin, prune, and harvest as needed.
  • Remove damaged material.
  • Prepare seeds before turnover.
Record changes, not just numbers.

Tell Pip what changed and when. Trends make better decisions than one isolated measurement.

Fast troubleshooting

Pause and check before adding more nutrients.

Identify delivery, roots, reservoir conditions, and recent changes first.

!

One tower is dryConfirm the pump, main hose, tower line, and top outlet before changing the full schedule.

!

Heavy runoffShorten the feed and recheck media moisture. More water is not automatically better.

!

Yellow or pale leavesCheck pH and EC/TDS after circulation before assuming more fertilizer is needed.

!

Brown, slimy, or foul rootsCheck reservoir temperature, circulation, debris, and light leaks promptly.

!

Green film or algaeFind where light reaches nutrient water, cover it, clean the area, and recheck.

!

Sudden system-wide changeLook for a shared cause: power, timer, pump, reservoir level, nutrients, or weather.

Harvest and reset

The next crop starts on flip day.

A tower turnover can be completed in a few focused hours when seedlings and supplies are ready.

  1. Harvest and identify healthy mature plants that can continue in raised beds.
  2. Lift the stacks off the removable top guide and center support.
  3. Shake plants over a screened tub to recover the perlite-vermiculite media.
  4. Remove roots, inspect the pots and feed lines, and flush where needed.
  5. Refill, restack, transplant the next starts, and record the crop date.
