How it works

One reservoir. A simple growing routine.

1

Build your towers

Support pipes, stackable planters, and reusable media form the growing space. Start with one tower or build four.

2

Mix and feed

One pump mixes the reservoir. A separate pump feeds the towers on a timer. There is no runoff-return plumbing.

3

Grow, harvest, repeat

Check your plants and flow, harvest what is ready, and reuse the media for the next crop. Pip helps with each next step.

A few practical answers

Find your starting point.

Can I start with fewer towers?

Yes. The preview supports one to four towers, and the member checklist supports custom sizes. Shared equipment means a single tower does not cost one quarter of a four-tower system.

Will it fit my space?

Plan about 12 × 8 ft for four towers, the reservoir, mature foliage, and working access. Check sunlight, water, and protected power before choosing the layout.

Open the placement guide
What do I need before assembly?

Gather the parts, confirm a safe reservoir history, and prepare the site. Before driving support pipes, call 811, wait for markings, and identify private utilities. Follow the Field Guide for water and electrical safety.
