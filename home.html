How it works

One reservoir. Brief feeds. Four towers growing real food.

HydroPip uses a large mixed reservoir and scheduled feed windows. Water reaches each tower from the top, moves through reusable media, and drains away without return plumbing.

Not sure what comes next?Pip can explain the system, check your plan, and guide the decision in front of you.

Mix one batch

Fill the 275-gallon IBC, choose strength from plant development, and circulate through the secured top-discharge hose.

2
Feed

A dedicated pump sends short scheduled doses through the main line.

3
Grow

Four vertical towers hold up to 160 planting pockets in reusable media.

4
Harvest + reset

Recover the media, preserve productive plants, and start the next crop.

Why HydroPip exists

Most beginner hydro systems make people manage too much.

Small recirculating systems can work, but new growers often end up chasing clogged lines, algae, return plumbing, unstable reservoirs, tiny pumps, and water that has been dragged back through the whole system. HydroPip takes a different path: feed the towers the right amount, from a large mixed reservoir, on a schedule Pip can help tune.

Build path

A simple system people can actually finish.

Start with the free build guide, then use Track My Build to keep every part, supply, and setup step organized. When a crop finishes, flipping pots, collecting reusable media, and replanting 160 pockets can also be done in a few focused hours.

1

Locate utilities, then drive the support post

Before driving any pipe, call 811 or start an 811 request online, wait for utility markings, and identify private irrigation, septic, electrical, or water lines. Then use an 8-10 foot, 1/2-inch galvanized steel pipe. Keep roughly 5 feet above grade; choose 10 feet for deeper anchoring in exposed or windier locations.

2

Stack the tower

Add the cinder block base and 10 four-pot planter sections. Each tower uses 2 planter orders.

3

Feed from the IBC

Use two pumps: one for circulation and one for scheduled tower feeding through the hose line.

4

Track the grow

Use Pip to tune feed timing, then come back for prune, harvest, media reset, and replanting reminders.

Tower turnover

Harvest today. Reset all 160 pockets in a few hours. Start the next crop.

A HydroPip harvest does not end with an empty tower. Lift off the pot stacks, move healthy mature plants into raised beds so they can keep producing, recover the reusable 50/50 perlite-vermiculite media, clear roots and debris, inspect the pots and feed line, then replant.

1Keep productive plants growingMove suitable mature plants into raised beds instead of treating every tower flip as waste.
2Recover the grow mediumShake plants over a screened collection tub, remove roots, and top off only the media that was lost.
3Restart in one focused sessionClean, inspect, stack, and replant while Pip helps prepare the next crop plan and schedule.
