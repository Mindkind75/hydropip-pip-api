A DIY hydroponic tower for home food production

Grow fresh vegetables at home without buying a proprietary tower.

HydroPip turns accessible parts into a commercial-quality, four-tower growing system. A 275-gallon reservoir supplies short scheduled feeds while reusable media supports greens, herbs, and suitable vegetables in up to 160 planting pockets.

1

Mix

Fill the IBC, choose the nutrient strength from plant development, and circulate one complete batch through the secured mixing hose.

2

Feed

A second pump sends short timed doses through the main hose and one small line per tower.

3

Grow

Ten four-pocket sections per tower hold up to 160 plants in reusable perlite and vermiculite.

4

Harvest + reset

Recover the media, move suitable mature plants to raised beds, refill the towers, and begin the next crop.

One tank. One nutrient batch. Adjust at the next refill. HydroPip does not use return plumbing. Start with short feeds, observe moisture and runoff, then let Pip help tune the schedule for the crop and conditions.

Common questions

What home growers want to know first.

Can this replace buying fresh vegetables?

It can meaningfully supplement greens, herbs, and suitable vegetables, but harvests depend on crops, climate, light, care, and planting rhythm. Start with foods your household already uses.

How much room does four towers need?

Plan approximately 12 by 8 feet for the standard working layout, including the IBC, mature foliage, and access to pumps and tower backs.

What does the system cost?

The current broad four-tower estimate is about $400-$550 before premium upgrades. Existing tools, pumps, supports, or a verified food-grade IBC can reduce the total.

Is this realistic for a beginner?

Yes, with ordinary tool skills and attention to safety. The free checklist organizes the parts, the Field Guide explains the method, and Pip can answer the question in front of you.

What should I grow first?

Leafy greens and herbs are strong starting crops. Fruiting plants can work but generally require more support, pruning, monitoring, and crop-specific planning.

Can I start with fewer towers?

Yes. HydroPip can scale from one tower and expand later. Track My Build adjusts quantities and costs for the selected tower count.
