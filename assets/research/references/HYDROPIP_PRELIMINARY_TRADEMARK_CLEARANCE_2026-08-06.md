# HYDROPIP Preliminary Trademark Clearance Search

Search date: August 6, 2026

## Scope and limitation

This is a preliminary owner-level clearance search, not a legal opinion or a substitute for a comprehensive search by a U.S. trademark attorney. It reviews exact and close federal records, selected related `PIP` records, public commercial use, Florida entity names, and major mobile app-store results.

## Proposed mark and likely services

- Proposed word mark: `HYDROPIP`
- Core use: hydroponic build education, online guidance, AI-assisted planning, software-as-a-service, grow records, schedules, and related affiliate product guidance
- Likely initial classes for attorney review: International Class 041 and International Class 042
- Possible later classes: International Class 009 for a downloadable app and International Class 035 if separately claimed retail or referral services justify it

## Federal search results

## Preliminary risk assessment

### Exact-name availability

Favorable. No exact federal `HYDROPIP` filing or competing exact commercial use was identified.

### Likelihood-of-confusion risk

Low to moderate, not zero. The complete `HYDROPIP` mark is distinctive as a whole, but `HYDRO` is suggestive or descriptive in this market, so the `PIP` portion may receive additional attention. Existing live `PIP` design registrations in irrigation equipment and Class 042 software consulting create issues that a filing attorney should assess before submission.

### Filing recommendation

Proceed to an attorney-reviewed federal application for the standard-character word mark `HYDROPIP`, provided counsel agrees after a professional comprehensive search. Draft the goods and services narrowly around HydroPip's actual hydroponic education and SaaS functions instead of claiming software or irrigation equipment broadly.

Recommended filing sequence:

1. File the standard-character `HYDROPIP` word mark.
2. Prioritize Classes 041 and 042 after confirming the exact identifications with counsel.
3. Add Class 009 when a downloadable mobile app is actually in use or has a supportable intent-to-use basis.
4. File a separate logo or mascot application only after one canonical HydroPip/Pip design is selected and used consistently.
5. Use `HydroPip(TM)` now and do not use the registered symbol until registration issues.
