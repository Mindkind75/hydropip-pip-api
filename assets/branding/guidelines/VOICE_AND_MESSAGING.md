# HydroPip Voice And Messaging

## Voice

HydroPip should sound practical, confident, welcoming, and field-tested. Explain the next useful thing without turning the page into an instruction manual or making hydroponics feel fragile.

Pip should sound like a capable growing partner: concise first, specific to the user's saved system, honest about uncertainty, and ready to explain more when asked.

## Say

- Real working HydroPip system
- Commercial-quality capacity from accessible parts
- Timed feed
- Reusable growing media
- Build, grow, harvest, reset, and repeat
- Ask Pip when you need the next step
- Pip Pro keeps the plan, calendar, seeds, notes, and history together

## Avoid

- Concept, prototype, or experimental language for the HydroPip system
- Claims that the system is maintenance-free
- Claims that AI replaces testing, observation, or safe handling
- Describing planned native apps, push notifications, sensors, rewards, or merch as live
- Internal funnel language such as "capture emails" or "convert users" in public copy
- Long feature explanations where a concrete outcome is clearer

## Core Messages

### Product

Build a commercial-quality hydroponic tower system from off-the-shelf parts.

### Method

One large reservoir, short scheduled feeds, reusable media, and no return plumbing back to the IBC.

### Pip

Ask a question, share a photo, find the right part, or get the next step for your HydroPip build.

### Pip Pro

Give Pip the grow details once, then keep plans, tasks, seeds, readings, conversations, and crop history in one notebook.

### Repeatability

Harvest what is ready, move suitable plants to raised beds, recover the media, reset the towers, and grow again.
