# HydroPip Beta Check-In Email

## Recommended Subject

HydroPip beta check-in: Pip has a lot more to show you

## Alternate Subjects

- New Pip Pro tools are ready for a test drive
- How is your HydroPip beta going?
- Pip got smarter. Want to put him to work?

## Preview Text

Smarter answers, saved conversations, photo guidance, planning, calendars, seeds, grow logs, and more are ready to test.

## Email Body

Hi {{first_name}},

Thank you again for helping test HydroPip. I wanted to check in, see how things are going, and invite you back for another test drive. The first version gave us a solid foundation, but Pip and the Pip Pro workspace have changed quite a bit since then.

**The short version:** Pip now remembers more about your grow, keeps conversations organized, accepts photos, and gives you a much more complete place to plan and manage a hydroponic system.

### What is new

- **Smarter, more personal answers:** Pip can use your saved growing zone, location, system, crops, growing area, and goals when answering.
- **Saved conversations:** Start separate conversations for crop planning, pests, maintenance, troubleshooting, or anything else, then continue them later from your phone or computer.
- **Photo guidance:** Show Pip a build step, plant, pest, root, plumbing problem, or piece of equipment and ask what he sees.
- **A complete Pip Pro notebook:** Profile, Planner, Calendar, Seeds, Grow Log, History, Track Build, Field Guide, Account, and Ask Pip are now connected in one workspace.
- **Planning and calendar tools:** Add planting, feeding, pruning, harvest, nutrient, and maintenance tasks, then see them in agenda, day, week, month, or year views.
- **Seeds and grow logs:** Track varieties, sowing dates, supplies, pH, EC/TDS, temperatures, observations, and results.
- **Build and parts help:** Track your HydroPip parts, use the printable checklist, and ask Pip for the correct product links when you need something.
- **Your workspace, your order:** Rearrange the notebook tabs to match how you work.
- **Better phone and tablet use:** Navigation, sizing, scrolling, and cross-device continuity have all received a major cleanup.

### Give it a quick test drive

You do not need to test everything at once. Even five or ten minutes helps:

1. Sign in and check that your grow profile is accurate.
2. Ask Pip a question that depends on your profile, such as what you should plant in your zone right now.
3. Start a new conversation or reopen an older one.
4. Upload a real photo and ask Pip about it.
5. Add one task, seed batch, or grow-log entry and make sure it appears where you expect.
6. Open the **Beta** tab in Pip Pro to see your testing checklist and send feedback.

**Open Pip Pro:** https://www.hydropip.com/pip?pro=1

The most useful feedback is simple and honest. Please tell us when an answer is wrong, a button does not work, something is confusing, or a feature does not behave the way you expected. A sentence or two is plenty. You can send it directly from the Beta tab, and you can choose whether to include the related question and Pip response.

Your beta access is complimentary, and you will not be charged while participating in the beta.

How has your experience been so far? You can reply directly to this email too. I would genuinely like to hear what you have built, what you are growing, and where HydroPip can help more.

Thank you for helping make Pip genuinely useful in the garden.

Edward  
HydroPip Systems  
https://www.hydropip.com
