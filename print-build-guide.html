Set the tower structure

Site, supports, media, and stack assembly

Page 2 of 4Build stages 1-3
Call before you drive. Call 811 or visit call811.com, wait for public utilities to be marked, and locate private irrigation, septic, electric, and water lines before driving any support.
Use a documented tote. Use an IBC tote only if its prior contents are documented as food-safe or non-hazardous and the tote has been cleaned appropriately. Do not use totes with unknown history, industrial chemicals, pesticides, petroleum products, solvents, cleaners, or other hazardous contents. When growing edible crops, choose food-contact-appropriate materials and wash produce before eating.
1

Choose the site

Confirm sun, drainage, working room, IBC access, outlet location, and a clear route for the main hose.

2

Lay out the towers

Mark four tower centers. Leave enough room to walk, harvest, lift stacks, and add future towers.

3

Drive each support

Use one 8-10 ft, 1/2 in galvanized steel pipe per tower. Keep roughly 5 ft above grade; anchor deeper where wind exposure requires it.

4

Set the base

Place and level one single-cell cinder block around each support pipe.

5

Mix the media

Combine perlite and vermiculite 50/50. Moisten lightly so filling is controlled.

6

Fill before stacking

Fill each four-pocket section evenly without packing the media hard.

7

Stack ten sections

Lower every section over the center support, rotate planting pockets for access, and keep the stack straight.

8

Add the top guide

Set a loose 3/4 in PVC tee over the top. It guides the hose and must lift off easily on turnover day.

Before plumbing

  • All four support pipes are stable and visibly straight.
  • Each cinder block sits level and does not rock.
  • Ten planter sections are installed on every tower.
  • Top tees can be removed without tools.
  • The IBC position leaves safe access to pumps, lid, and hoses.

Site measurements / changes

Plumb, mix, and test

IBC pumps, feed line, tower tubes, and first run

Page 3 of 4Build stages 4-6
1

Secure the pumps

Zip-tie the two submersible pumps together so they do not spin or separate inside the IBC.

2

Install the mixing-pump circulation hose

Place the mixing pump low, attach correctly sized flexible hose, run it to the large top opening, bend it near the opening, and point the outlet down inside. Secure it to the molded anchor loops with a bungee, rope, or reusable strap. Aim for visible broad circulation without splashing. Tower runoff is not plumbed back to the IBC, and the tote is not drilled.

3

Run the main feed hose

Connect the second pump to the main garden hose and route it through every loose top tee.

4

Add tower branches

Punch one clean hole at each tower and insert a rigid 1/4 in feed tube into the top center of the stack.

5

Add diffusers

Use short diffuser pieces so water spreads into the media instead of striking one hard spot.

6

Finish the line

Add the hose-end shutoff/flush fitting and connector that allows future tower expansion.

7

Flush before planting

Open the end valve, run the feed pump briefly, clear debris, close it, and inspect every connection.

8

Confirm equal delivery

Run clean water and verify every tower receives a controlled top feed without leaks or heavy runoff.

Mix one complete IBC batch

  • Fill with approximately 275 gallons of clean water.
  • Start the low mixing pump and confirm its secured hose discharges at the top opening.
  • Dissolve magnesium sulfate, then MasterBlend, then calcium nitrate separately.
  • Mix until even; record the plant stage and grams used.
  • pH is useful; EC/TDS is optional optimization.

Seeds 300/300/150. Growing 400/400/200. Fruiting 600/600/300.

Start the timer

  • Use separate smart-plug schedules for mixing and feeding.
  • Begin with short daytime feed windows.
  • Check one representative tower.
  • Tune using media moisture and light runoff.
  • Do not copy a universal runtime.

Plant, run, and reset

The first crop and the repeatable turnover cycle

Page 4 of 4Grow reference

First planting

  • Choose crops for the season and grow zone.
  • Transplant into evenly moist media.
  • Record crop, variety, and planting date.
  • Confirm every tower receives water.
  • Watch plants and media before increasing feeds.

Routine system check

  • IBC level, temperature, and light cover
  • Secured mixing-pump top discharge and pump intake
  • pH if measured; EC/TDS is optional
  • Feed timer, leaks, tower flow, media, and runoff
  • Leaves, roots, pests, pruning, and harvest

Stop and investigate

  • One tower suddenly runs dry
  • Heavy runoff or constantly soaked media
  • Mixing-pump circulation is weak, unsecured, or splashing outside
  • Brown, slimy, or foul-smelling roots
  • Green film, algae, pump heat, or unusual noise

Tower turnover

  1. Harvest and move healthy mature plants into raised beds when they can continue producing.
  2. Lift the loose top guide and slide planter sections off the center support.
  3. Shake plants over a screened storage tub to recover reusable media.
  4. Remove roots, inspect pots and feed lines, and flush or clean where needed.
  5. Top off the recovered 50/50 media, refill the sections, and restack.
  6. Transplant the next crop and record the new planting date.
